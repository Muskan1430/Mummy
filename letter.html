My Dearest Mummy 💌



There will never be enough words to thank you for everything you’ve done for me. You gave up your dreams so I could chase mine. You hid your pain behind smiles just to make sure I was happy. You stayed strong even when life was difficult, and you loved me with all your heart every single day.

I still remember all the little moments that meant the world to me. When I was ill, you stayed awake the whole night beside me, making sure I was okay. When I failed or doubted myself, you were the one who said, “My child can do anything.” When my heart was broken, your hug made me feel safe again in a way nobody else ever could.

No matter how old I grow, I will always need your love, your guidance, and your comforting presence. You are my biggest blessing, my safe place, and the purest form of love I have ever known.

Mom, I promise:
- To make you proud every single day
- To always respect and love you
- To be there for you like you were for me
- To give you all the happiness you deserve

Happy Mother's Day to the World's Best Mom!
Your loving child,
Forever Yours 💕
